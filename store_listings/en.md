## full (≤4000)
Snake Classic is the retro snake game you grew up with, rebuilt as a modern arcade game. Steer, eat, grow and don't hit your own tail. One more run is always a swipe away. Play offline anywhere, or jump into online 1v1 matches.

RETRO ARCADE GAMEPLAY
• Smooth 60 FPS movement with responsive swipe controls
• Optional on-screen D-pad, turn buttons or joystick, plus keyboard support
• Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
• Nine board sizes, from a tight 15x15 to a huge 50x50
• Combos multiply your score up to 3x while you keep eating
• Golden apples and star food for bonus points

8 GAME MODES
• Classic: the classic snake game with walls
• Zen Mode: no walls, wrap around the screen edges
• Speed Challenge: the speed climbs fast, built for quick reflexes
• Multi-Food: several foods on the board at once
• Survival: last as long as you can with limited lives
• Time Attack: a speed run for the highest score against the clock
• Power-Up Madness: power-ups spawn far more often
• Perfect Game: never cross your own trail

POWER-UPS
Speed Boost, Invincibility, Score Multiplier and Slow Motion appear on the board and can turn any run around.

ONLINE 1V1 MULTIPLAYER
Quick Match finds you an opponent automatically, or create a private room and share the code with a friend. Duels are played on classic rules, and wins earn coins.

TOURNAMENTS, CHALLENGES AND THE BATTLE PASS
• Daily, weekly and monthly tournaments
• Three new daily challenges every day
• A 100-tier battle pass with a free reward track for everyone
• 147 achievements across five rarities
• Global and weekly leaderboards
• Player levels, a daily login bonus and detailed statistics

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from 10 board themes, including Retro, Neon, Cyberpunk and Space, then unlock 12 snake skins and 11 glowing trail effects.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Single-player works fully offline, no Wi-Fi needed
• Play as a guest, or sign in with Google to back up your progress and sync it across devices
• Rewatch your best runs with frame-by-frame replays
• Add friends and see who is online
• Available in 9 languages

GO PRO
An optional monthly or yearly subscription: no ads, every premium theme, skin and trail, the premium battle pass track, double coins, a free revive in every game, and tournament entries each billing cycle.

Snake Classic is free to play and includes ads and optional in-app purchases.
