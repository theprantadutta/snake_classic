## full (≤4000)
Snake Classic brings the timeless snake game to life with premium visuals, buttery-smooth 60 FPS gameplay, and more ways to play than ever.

EAT. GROW. SURVIVE.
Swipe (or use the on-screen D-Pad) to steer your snake, eat food to grow, and dodge walls and your own tail. Simple to pick up, hard to put down.

8 GAME MODES
• Classic — the original walls-on experience
• Zen — no walls, no pressure
• Speed Challenge — a fast tick for quick reflexes
• MultiFood — three foods on the board at once
• Survival — three lives, rising speed
• Time Attack — score as much as you can in 3 minutes
• Power-Up Madness — frequent power-ups everywhere
• Perfect Game — never cross your own trail

PLAY ONLINE
Face other players in real-time 1v1 matches, climb the leaderboards, and compete in daily and weekly tournaments.

PROGRESS & REWARDS
• 107 achievements to unlock, from First Bite to Score God
• Daily challenges and weekly quests that stack up coins
• A battle pass season track with free rewards for everyone
• Player levels, streaks, statistics, and personal bests

MAKE IT YOURS
Unlock snake skins, glowing trail effects, and board themes. Choose your board size, difficulty, and controls.

BUILT RIGHT
• Offline-first: play anywhere, your progress syncs when you're back online
• Cloud save across devices
• Replays of your best runs
• Available in 9 languages
