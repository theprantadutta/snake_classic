# Release notes — v5.0.0

Paste-ready "What's new" text for Play Console / App Store Connect
(each block is under the 500-character Play limit).

## en
🌍 Snake Classic now speaks 9 languages! English, हिन्दी, Português, Español, Français, Русский, Polski, العربية and Italiano — pick yours in Settings.
🎮 Online 1v1 got a big upgrade: faster matchmaking, automatic reconnect if you drop, and win/loss records with skill-based ratings.
🔔 Challenges, tournaments and notifications now arrive in your language.
🏅 40 new achievements to unlock — 147 in total!
🐛 Plus lots of bug fixes and polish. Thanks for playing!
